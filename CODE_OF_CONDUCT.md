# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes respect, constructive criticism, and welcoming different opinions. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, and disrespectful conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to project maintainers, who will review complaints fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may issue warnings or temporarily or permanently exclude individuals depending on the severity of violations.
